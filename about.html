2022 – 2025

I graduated with a BBA, specialising in Marketing, from Central University of Karnataka. But if I'm honest, my real education has been slower and messier than that — built from books I couldn't put down, campaigns I found myself dissecting, and a growing curiosity about what actually makes people stop and pay attention.

Marketing, at its best, is applied psychology. It's understanding that people don't buy products — they buy feelings, identities, solutions to problems they haven't fully articulated yet. That's what I find endlessly interesting. Not just the tactics, but the thinking behind them.

I'm early in my career and I know it. But I'd rather be someone who reads widely, asks better questions, and keeps showing up — than someone who pretends to have it all figured out. This blog is part of that showing up.
